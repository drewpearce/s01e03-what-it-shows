Why the merge hid them

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone. A subject says the date is wrong, not why: 10 of the 12 subject lines in #4471 appear under both parser and hint. Intake tags did not help either: they are a guess from the reporter's browser, and 38 reports carry the wrong one.

For the desk

  • Count by cause (tenant locale, form, channel), not by subject or intake tag.
  • Read the body before merging when the symptom is "one day off": that is not a swap.
